Teaching a Dominion player to buy better by searching through the shuffles it can't see

A light version of AlphaZero for the card game Dominion (base set, Steam rules). At every buy it runs an information-set Monte Carlo tree search: each of its 400 simulations deals the hidden cards anew (its own draw order, the opponent's hand and deck), so the search averages over the luck of the draw instead of guessing it. A small neural network guides the search and learns from it in self-play. The goal: win 65% of 1,000 fresh games head to head against the evolved GA genome it started from (same play of action cards; only the buys differ).
